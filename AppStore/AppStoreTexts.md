### Description (max 4000)

Ding! Service Bell turns your iPhone into the classic brass bell from a hotel reception desk.

Tap the bell and it rings with a real recording of a service bell – loud, bright and impossible to ignore. Tap as fast as you like: like a real bell, every ring overlaps the last. A gentle haptic tap lets you feel each ring.

And because no one likes a bell that won't stop, Service Bell does something a real bell can't: tap the counter anywhere around it and the ringing fades out at once.

DESIGNED TO LOOK REAL
• A polished brass dome that mirrors the lamplight like real metal
• A turned wooden base painted black
• A varnished walnut counter with a warm lamp glow
• A knob that sinks in when you press it and springs back

MADE TO WORK
• Rings even when your iPhone is set to silent
• Plays along with your music instead of stopping it
• The screen stays on while the app is open, so the bell is always ready
• Works offline

PERFECT FOR
• David Parr's "Copy Cat" routine
• Game nights, quizzes and classroom buzzers

NO STRINGS ATTACHED
No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Service Bell collects no data at all.

### App Review notes

No login or setup needed. Tap the brass bell to ring it; tap anywhere outside the bell (the wooden counter) to fade the sound out. The sound plays even with the silent switch on, because ringing is the app's only purpose. The app makes no network requests and collects no data. Ding! Service Bell verwandelt dein iPhone in die klassische Messingglocke von der Hotelrezeption.

Tippe auf die Glocke und sie läutet mit der echten Aufnahme einer Tresenglocke – laut, hell und nicht zu überhören. Tippe so schnell du willst: Wie bei einer echten Glocke überlagert sich jedes Läuten mit dem vorigen. Ein sanftes haptisches Feedback lässt dich jedes Läuten spüren.

Und weil niemand eine Glocke mag, die nicht aufhört, kann Service Bell etwas, das keine echte Glocke kann: Tippe irgendwo auf die Theke und das Läuten verklingt sofort.
